# Welcome to My Notes 👋

This is a **sample vault**: a small, made-up set of notes for **Northwind Bakery**, a client of a small web studio. Play with it as much as you like. When you're ready, click **Open vault…** (bottom left) and choose your own notes folder. Your own notes are never mixed with these.

## Try these first
1. Open [[Website relaunch plan]]. Set its **importance**, add a **tag**, look at its **properties** (status, client, due).
2. Type `[[` anywhere and pick a note to link it. The linked note shows this one under **Backlinks**.
3. Press **Ctrl+K** and search for `northwind`, or `folder:Clients`, or `status:Doing`.
4. Press **Ctrl+O** to jump to a note by name, and **Ctrl+Shift+A** to see every command.
5. Open the **Tasks** table (in *Projects*): edit cells, add a row with **Ctrl+Enter**, filter, sort.
6. Open [[Website relaunch (map)]] and switch to **Map** view: cards and arrows made from plain text.
7. Click the **graph** button at the top to see how the notes link together.
8. Press **Ctrl+D** for today's daily note (folder *DAILY*).
9. Click the **lock** button next to a note's title to encrypt it with a password.

## AI (optional)
Click **✨** next to a note's title; the first time, My Notes asks for your own Claude API key (from console.anthropic.com). Then try the **Suggestions** and the **Ask** row, select a paragraph and click the ✨ that appears, or press **Ctrl+J** and ask *"What did we decide for the Northwind website?"*
